Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?

A:
Go to Login page
Click Forgot Password
Follow the email instructions.

Q: How do I update my profile?

A:
Go to Account Settings
Click Edit Profile.

Q: How do I download invoices?

A:
Go to Billing
Select Invoice History.

Q: How do I activate my account?

A:
Click the activation link sent to your registered email.
